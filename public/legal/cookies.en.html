Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026 · Français

1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small file or piece of data stored in your browser. It is used to make a site work or to measure its use. The same principles apply to local storage data (localStorage), which we use to remember your choices.

2. Cookies and data used on this site

NameTypePurposeDuration
teknic-cookie-consent Local storage (essential) Remember your cookie choice (accepted or refused). Until you change it or clear your browsing data.
teknic-lang Local storage (essential) Remember your language choice (French or English). Until you change it or clear your browsing data.
Google Maps cookies Third-party cookies (non-essential) Display the map of our shop on the Contact page. Google may use these cookies according to its own policy. According to Google's policy.
_ga, _ga_* Third-party cookies (Google Analytics, non-essential) Count visits and understand which pages are viewed. Enabled only if you accept. Up to 2 years.

Visit statistics: if you accept cookies, we use Google Analytics to know how many people visit the site and which pages are viewed. These statistics are aggregated and are not used for advertising. We do not use any advertising cookies.

3. Essential and non-essential cookies

  • Essential: required for the site to work. They do not require consent.
  • Non-essential: used for the embedded map or other features. They are enabled only if you accept them.

4. Managing your choices

On your first visit, a banner lets you accept or refuse non-essential cookies. You can change your choice at any time with the "Manage cookies" link at the bottom of each page.

You can also manage cookies in your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge
